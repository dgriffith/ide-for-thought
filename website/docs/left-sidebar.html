Docs/Left sidebar

Left sidebar

The left sidebar is your workspace rail — five panels, each a different way to look at your project. Switch between them with the row of tab icons at the top of the sidebar.

The five panels

Notes tree
The main panel — your notes and folders, and where you create, rename, and move them.
Sources
Web pages, PDFs, and references you've saved — kept apart from your notes, but easy to cite from them.
Tags
An alphabetical list of every tag in your project, so you can jump to everything sharing a label.
Tables
The data tables in your project, ready to browse and search.
Bookmarks
Spots you've saved yourself — a whole note, a section, or an exact line — kept in folders of your own.
