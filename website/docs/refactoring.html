Refactoring

Rename, move, and delete notes — either by hand or by asking the assistant to do it for you. Whichever way you go, the links between your notes stay coherent, because Minerva keeps them up to date for you.

Deleting is a normal operation

Renaming, moving, and deleting are everyday actions, not scary ones. Minerva asks for a simple confirmation and then gets out of your way.
