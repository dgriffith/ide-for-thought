Docs/Conversations

Conversations

Conversations is where you think alongside the AI instead of editing everything by hand. You chat, it reads your notes as it works, and when it wants to change something — add a claim, rewrite a note, suggest a tag — it hands you a suggestion instead of just doing it. One rule holds throughout: the AI proposes, you decide. Nothing changes your notes until you approve it.

Treat what the AI offers as a suggestion to weigh and file, not a decision already made. This is the most important idea in Minerva — see The propose → review → approve loop for the full picture.
