Export

When you want to take your work out of Minerva, exporting gets it into a shape you can share or reuse — a document, a website, citation files, or study cards. Every export lets you choose what to include before you run it.

At a glance

—
Document exports — turn a note into a shareable Markdown, web page, or PDF file, on its own or bundled with the notes it links to.
—
Publishing — turn your notes into a browseable website and put it online.
—
Bibliography & citations — export your references as citation files, and set the citation style that shapes them.
—
Flashcards — build a deck of study cards from the cards you mark in your notes.
—
Scopes & privacy — what an export includes, and what Minerva holds back by default.
