Learning Operations

The Learning menu holds skills for understanding something already in front of you — re-explaining it at your level, drawing out examples and prerequisites, building study material, and testing yourself. Each runs over the current note (or a selection) and hands back a suggestion you review before anything is kept.

The skills

Explain Like I’m…
Re-explain a topic at a chosen audience level.
Summarize
Open a conversation that summarizes the active note.
Give Examples
Generate concrete examples illustrating the note.
Find Counterexamples
Where does this note’s argument break down?
Find Prerequisites
List concepts to understand before tackling this note.
Create Learning Journey
Design an ordered learning path ending at mastery.
Generate Glossary
Build a structured, graph-typed glossary for a note or topic.
Add Term to Glossary
Add one term to the existing glossary, matching its conventions.
Deep Dive on Term
Expand a selected term into a fuller explanation.
Quiz Me
Test your understanding of the note with graded questions.
Propose Flashcards
Draft atomic spaced-repetition cards from the note, for your review.
These are the stock skills

This is what ships in the Learning menu. You can turn skills on and off, move them between menus, reorder them, or write your own — see Managing & authoring.
