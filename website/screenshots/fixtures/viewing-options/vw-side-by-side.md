# The Bowlback Mandolin

The **Neapolitan** bowlback is the instrument most people picture when they
hear the word *mandolin*: a deep, staved bowl of rosewood ribs, a canted
soundboard, and eight strings in four courses.

## Tuning

The courses are tuned in fifths, exactly like a violin:

- G – D – A – E, low to high
- Each course is a matched pair, played as one

> "The tremolo is the mandolin's singing voice — a fast alternation of the
> pick that turns plucked notes into a sustained line."
