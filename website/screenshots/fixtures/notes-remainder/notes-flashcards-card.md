# Mandolin Flashcards

Study cards live right inside a note. The front shows on its own with a
**Show answer** button, so you can test yourself before revealing the back.

> [!card] Mandolin deck
> How many strings does a standard mandolin have, and how are they arranged?
> ---
> **Eight strings** in **four courses** of two, tuned in unison per course to
> G–D–A–E — the same intervals as a violin.

> [!card] Mandolin deck
> What playing technique gives the mandolin its singing, sustained melody line?
> ---
> The **tremolo** — rapid alternate picking across a course that fills the gap
> between a plucked note's quick decay.
