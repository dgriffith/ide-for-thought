# A Mandolin Performance

Drop a YouTube link into a note and it renders as a tidy poster card — the
thumbnail with a play button. Click the card to open the video in your browser.

```youtube
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88q9syere0Q
Chris Thile plays "Jessamyn's Reel" on mandolin
```

The link stays clean and portable; nothing third-party loads inside your notes.
