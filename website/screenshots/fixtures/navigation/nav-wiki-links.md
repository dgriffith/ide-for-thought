# Following the mandolin's trail

The story of the instrument is a web of linked notes. From the parlor
to the barn dance, every thread connects to another.

Bill Monroe carried the Neapolitan bowl-back into the hills and gave us
[[The Rise of Bluegrass]] — a chopping, percussive style that reshaped
American string bands for good.

For the acoustics behind that bright, cutting tone, see
[[The Physics of the Mandolin]], where the arched top and paired courses
do their quiet work.

Follow any link to jump straight to the note it names, or rest your
pointer on one to peek at where it leads.
