# Tuning the Mandolin

The mandolin is tuned in **perfect fifths** — G–D–A–E — exactly like a
_violin_, which is why fiddle players feel ==instantly at home== on it.

Each course is a **pair of strings** tuned in unison, giving the instrument
its bright, ringing voice. See [[Mandolin Family Tree]] for how that tuning
travels across the wider family.
