# Vienna

Vienna houses a large share of its residents in city-owned and limited-profit
*Gemeindebau* and cooperative housing. Rents there are set outside the private
market, which is often cited as proof that the price mechanism is not the only
route to affordability.

The interesting question for [[notes/upzoning-lowers-rents]] is whether Vienna
is a *counterexample* to the supply argument or a *confirmation* of it by other
means — the city also builds a great deal, sustaining a high effective
[[notes/glossary/elasticity-of-supply|supply elasticity]].
